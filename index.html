Chapter 1: Percentageप्रतिशत  |  Useful for: NTPC • Group D • Technician • CGL • CHSL

VERY HIGH PRIORITY

1. Concept / अवधारणा

Percentage means 'per hundred'. x% = x/100. It is the base of Profit-Loss, Interest, DI, Population and Averages, so master this first.

प्रतिशत का अर्थ है 'प्रति सैकड़ा'। x% = x/100। यह लाभ-हानि, ब्याज, DI और औसत की नींव है, इसलिए सबसे पहले इसे पक्का करें।

📐 FORMULA BOX / सूत्र
  • x% of y = (x × y)/100   |   x% of y = y% of x
  • Net change after +a% and +b% = a + b + (a×b)/100  (use minus sign for decrease)
  • Increase x%, then decrease to return to original = x/(100+x) × 100 %
  • Decrease x%, then increase to return to original = x/(100−x) × 100 %
  • If A is x% more than B, then B is less than A by = x/(100+x) × 100 %
  • If A is x% less than B, then B is more than A by = x/(100−x) × 100 %
  • Price up x% ⇒ consumption must fall by x/(100+x) × 100 % to keep expenditure same
  • Population after n years = P (1 + r/100)n
  • Side of square ↑ a% ⇒ area changes by a + a + a²/100 %; same rule for radius of circle

🧠 Fraction ↔ Percent table (must memorise)

⚡ 10-SECOND TRICKS / शॉर्टकट
  • Learn the fraction table by heart. 12.5% = 1/8 means 'divide by 8' — no long multiplication. / भिन्न-प्रतिशत तालिका याद रखें।
  • Assume base = 100 for every 'percent change' question (Normal method). / प्रतिशत परिवर्तन में आधार 100 मान लें।
  • x% of y = y% of x → 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4. / 8% of 50 को 50% of 8 बनाकर तुरंत हल करें।
  • Successive changes: never add percentages directly; use a + b + ab/100.
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES / सामान्य गलतियाँ
  • Adding +20% and −20% to get 0% (actual: −4%).
  • Using the NEW value as base when asked 'by what % is B less than A' (base is always the one after 'than').
  • Forgetting to convert fraction table values in 1/7 and 1/9 type questions.
⚠️ EXAM TRAPS / परीक्षा जाल
  • 'A is 25% more than B' ≠ 'B is 25% less than A' (B is 20% less).
  • Net change signs: a decrease must be put with a minus sign in the formula.

2. Solved Examples — Normal vs Shortcut / हल उदाहरण

Ex 1.1 A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. Find the net percentage change.
NORMAL METHOD: Number = 100 → after +20% = 120 → after −20% = 96. Net = 4% decrease.
SHORTCUT METHOD: Net = a + b + ab/100 = 20 − 20 + (20×−20)/100 = −4%.
Answer: 4% decrease
Ex 1.2 Price of sugar rises by 25%. By what % must a family cut consumption to keep expenditure unchanged?
NORMAL METHOD: Old price 100, new 125. Expenditure same ⇒ new quantity = 100/125 = 80% of old ⇒ cut = 20%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: x/(100+x) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%.
Answer: 20%
Ex 1.3 A's salary is 25% more than B's. B's salary is what % less than A's?
NORMAL METHOD: B = 100, A = 125. Difference = 25 on base A (125) → 25/125 = 20%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: x/(100+x) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%.
Answer: 20%
Ex 1.4 40% of a number is 84. Find the number.
NORMAL METHOD: 40% = 84 ⇒ 1% = 2.1 ⇒ 100% = 210.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 40% = 2/5. So (2/5) × N = 84 ⇒ N = 84 × 5/2 = 210.
Answer: 210
Ex 1.5 A student needs 40% marks to pass. He gets 125 marks and fails by 15. Find the maximum marks.
NORMAL METHOD: Pass marks = 125 + 15 = 140. 40% of M = 140 ⇒ M = 140 × 100/40 = 350.
SHORTCUT METHOD: Pass marks 140 = 2/5 of M ⇒ M = 140 × 5/2 = 350.
Answer: 350

Chapter 2: Profit, Loss & Discountलाभ, हानि और छूट  |  Useful for: NTPC • Group D • Technician • CGL • CHSL

VERY HIGH PRIORITY

1. Concept / अवधारणा

Profit/Loss is always calculated on Cost Price (CP). Discount is always calculated on Marked Price (MP). Selling Price (SP) = what the customer pays.

लाभ/हानि हमेशा क्रय मूल्य (CP) पर निकलता है। छूट हमेशा अंकित मूल्य (MP) पर निकलती है। विक्रय मूल्य (SP) = ग्राहक द्वारा दिया गया मूल्य।

📐 FORMULA BOX / सूत्र
  • Profit = SP − CP  |  Loss = CP − SP
  • Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100  |  Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
  • SP = CP × (100 + P%)/100  |  CP = SP × 100/(100 + P%)
  • For loss: SP = CP × (100 − L%)/100  |  CP = SP × 100/(100 − L%)
  • Discount = MP − SP  |  Discount % = (Discount/MP) × 100
  • SP = MP × (100 − D%)/100
  • Two successive discounts a% and b% = a + b − (a×b)/100 %
  • Same SP, one article at x% gain, another at x% loss ⇒ always LOSS = x²/100 %
  • Dishonest dealer (false weight): Gain % = Error/(True weight − Error) × 100
⚡ 10-SECOND TRICKS / शॉर्टकट
  • CP : SP = 100 : (100 + P) — write ratios and cross-multiply. / अनुपात बनाकर हल करें।
  • Assume CP = 100 (or LCM-friendly value like 1000) for percentage-only questions.
  • Marked 40% above CP, discount 10% ⇒ multiply 1.4 × 0.9 = 1.26 ⇒ 26% profit.
  • 'Cost of x articles = SP of y articles' ⇒ Profit % = (x − y)/y × 100.
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES / सामान्य गलतियाँ
  • Calculating profit % on SP instead of CP.
  • Calculating discount % on CP instead of MP.
  • Adding two discounts directly (20% + 10% ≠ 30%).
⚠️ EXAM TRAPS / परीक्षा जाल
  • 'Same SP, equal gain and loss %' always gives a loss, never 'no profit no loss'.
  • Free items offers (buy 3 get 1 free) = discount of 1/4 = 25%.

2. Solved Examples — Normal vs Shortcut / हल उदाहरण

Ex 2.1 An article bought for ₹500 is sold for ₹600. Find the profit %.
NORMAL METHOD: Profit = 100. Profit % = 100/500 × 100 = 20%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: SP/CP = 600/500 = 6/5 = 1.2 ⇒ 20% profit.
Answer: 20%
Ex 2.2 An article sold at ₹720 gives a 20% loss. Find its CP.
NORMAL METHOD: SP = 80% of CP ⇒ CP = 720 × 100/80 = ₹900.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 80% = 4/5 ⇒ CP = 720 × 5/4 = ₹900.
Answer: ₹900
Ex 2.3 Find the single discount equal to successive discounts of 20% and 10%.
NORMAL METHOD: Price 100 → after 20% = 80 → after 10% = 72. Net discount = 28%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 20 + 10 − (20×10)/100 = 28%.
Answer: 28%
Ex 2.4 Two articles are each sold at ₹1,200; one at 20% gain and the other at 20% loss. Find overall result.
NORMAL METHOD: CP₁ = 1200×100/120 = 1000; CP₂ = 1200×100/80 = 1500. Total CP = 2500, SP = 2400 ⇒ loss 100 ⇒ 4%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: Loss % = x²/100 = 20²/100 = 4%.
Answer: 4% loss
Ex 2.5 Shopkeeper marks goods 40% above CP and allows 10% discount. Find profit %.
NORMAL METHOD: CP = 100, MP = 140, SP = 140 × 0.9 = 126 ⇒ profit 26%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 1.4 × 0.9 = 1.26 ⇒ 26%.
Answer: 26%

HIGH PRIORITY Same SP, equal gain & loss % (loss = x²/100).

HIGH PRIORITY 'CP of x articles = SP of y articles'.

Chapter 3: Simple & Compound Interestसाधारण एवं चक्रवृद्धि ब्याज  |  Useful for: NTPC • Group D • CGL • CHSL

VERY HIGH PRIORITY

1. Concept / अवधारणा

Simple Interest (SI) is calculated on the original principal every year. Compound Interest (CI) is calculated on principal + earned interest.

साधारण ब्याज सदैव मूलधन पर लगता है। चक्रवृद्धि ब्याज मूलधन + पिछले ब्याज पर लगता है।

📐 FORMULA BOX / सूत्र
  • SI = (P × R × T)/100  |  Amount A = P + SI
  • CI Amount = P (1 + R/100)n  |  CI = A − P
  • Half-yearly compounding: Rate = R/2, Time = 2n
  • Quarterly compounding: Rate = R/4, Time = 4n
  • CI − SI for 2 years = P (R/100)2
  • CI − SI for 3 years = P (R/100)2 (3 + R/100)
  • Sum becomes k times in T years at SI ⇒ R = (k − 1) × 100 / T
  • Effective 2-year rate in CI = R + R + R²/100 %
⚡ 10-SECOND TRICKS / शॉर्टकट
  • 2-year CI at R%: use successive-percentage rule: R + R + R²/100. For 10%: 10 + 10 + 1 = 21%. / 2 वर्ष का CI = 21% (10% दर पर)।
  • CI − SI (2 yrs) = P × (R/100)². For ₹5000 at 10%: 5000 × 0.01 = ₹50.
  • If SI doubles money in T years, it triples in 2T years (rate stays same).
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES / सामान्य गलतियाँ
  • Using the CI formula for 'annually' when the question says 'half-yearly'.
  • Forgetting that CI asked ≠ Amount asked (subtract P).
  • Using R = given rate for the half-yearly case (must be R/2).
⚠️ EXAM TRAPS / परीक्षा जाल
  • Difference questions: SI is the same every year, CI second year onward is larger.
  • Time in months/days must be converted into years.

2. Solved Examples — Normal vs Shortcut / हल उदाहरण

Ex 3.1 Find the SI on ₹5,000 at 8% p.a. for 3 years.
NORMAL METHOD: SI = 5000 × 8 × 3/100 = ₹1,200.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 1% of 5000 = 50; 50 × 8 × 3 = ₹1,200.
Answer: ₹1,200
Ex 3.2 Find the CI on ₹10,000 at 10% p.a. for 2 years.
NORMAL METHOD: Year 1: 10,000 → 11,000. Year 2: 11,000 → 12,100. CI = 2,100.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 2-year rate = 10 + 10 + 1 = 21% ⇒ 21% of 10,000 = ₹2,100.
Answer: ₹2,100
Ex 3.3 Find CI − SI on ₹5,000 at 10% for 2 years.
NORMAL METHOD: SI = 5000×10×2/100 = 1,000. CI = 21% of 5000 = 1,050. Difference = ₹50.
SHORTCUT METHOD: P (R/100)² = 5000 × 0.01 = ₹50.
Answer: ₹50
Ex 3.4 At simple interest a sum doubles in 5 years. Find the rate.
NORMAL METHOD: SI = P in 5 years ⇒ P × R × 5/100 = P ⇒ R = 20%.
SHORTCUT METHOD: R = (k−1) × 100/T = 100/5 = 20%.
Answer: 20% p.a.
Ex 3.5 Find the CI on ₹8,000 at 10% p.a. for 1 year, compounded half-yearly.
NORMAL METHOD: Rate 5%, 2 periods: 8000 → 8400 → 8820. CI = 820.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 2-period rate = 5 + 5 + 0.25 = 10.25% ⇒ 10.25% of 8000 = ₹820.
Answer: ₹820

HIGH PRIORITY CI for 2/3 years on a given sum at a given rate.

Chapter 4: Time & Work (with Pipes & Cisterns)समय और कार्य (नल एवं टंकी सहित)  |  Useful for: NTPC • Group D • Technician • CGL • CHSL

VERY HIGH PRIORITY

1. Concept / अवधारणा

Work = Efficiency × Time. If A completes a job in 10 days, A's 1-day work = 1/10. More efficiency = less time (inverse relation).

कार्य = कार्यक्षमता × समय। यदि A काम 10 दिन में करता है तो A का 1 दिन का काम = 1/10। कार्यक्षमता अधिक ⇒ समय कम।

📐 FORMULA BOX / सूत्र
  • Work = Efficiency × Time
  • A and B together = (a × b)/(a + b) days
  • Efficiency ratio = inverse of time ratio
  • M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂ (men–days–hours chain)
  • Pipes: inlet = +ve work, outlet = −ve work. Net rate = sum of rates
  • Wages are divided in the ratio of work done (efficiency × days)
⚡ 10-SECOND TRICKS / शॉर्टकट
  • LCM method: take total work = LCM of days. Each person's daily work = LCM ÷ days. / कुल कार्य = दिनों का LCM।
  • A 10 days, B 15 days: LCM 30 → A = 3/day, B = 2/day → together 5/day → 30/5 = 6 days.
  • Pipe fills in 12 h and drain empties in 18 h: LCM 36 → +3 −2 = +1 → 36 hours.
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES / सामान्य गलतियाँ
  • Adding days directly (10 + 15) instead of adding rates.
  • Forgetting to subtract outlet pipes.
  • Using different units (days vs hours) in one equation.
⚠️ EXAM TRAPS / परीक्षा जाल
  • 'Alternate days' questions: calculate one 2-day cycle first.
  • Leaving-before-completion questions: subtract the days they worked, not the total.

2. Solved Examples — Normal vs Shortcut / हल उदाहरण

Ex 4.1 A can do a work in 10 days, B in 15 days. In how many days will they finish together?
NORMAL METHOD: A's 1-day = 1/10, B's = 1/15. Together = 1/10 + 1/15 = 5/30 = 1/6 ⇒ 6 days.
SHORTCUT METHOD: LCM = 30. A = 3, B = 2. Together 5/day ⇒ 30/5 = 6 days.
Answer: 6 days
Ex 4.2 A and B together finish in 12 days; A alone in 20 days. How long will B alone take?
NORMAL METHOD: B's 1-day = 1/12 − 1/20 = (5−3)/60 = 1/30 ⇒ 30 days.
SHORTCUT METHOD: LCM = 60. Together 5/day, A = 3/day ⇒ B = 2/day ⇒ 60/2 = 30 days.
Answer: 30 days
Ex 4.3 A can finish in 10 days, B in 15 days. A works for 4 days and leaves. In how many days will B finish the rest?
NORMAL METHOD: A's 4-day work = 4/10 = 2/5. Left = 3/5. B's days = (3/5) ÷ (1/15) = 9 days.
SHORTCUT METHOD: LCM = 30; A = 3, B = 2. Done = 12; left 18 ⇒ 18/2 = 9 days.
Answer: 9 days
Ex 4.4 15 men complete a work in 20 days. How many extra men are needed to finish in 12 days?
NORMAL METHOD: Total = 15 × 20 = 300 man-days. Men needed = 300/12 = 25. Extra = 10.
SHORTCUT METHOD: M₁D₁ = M₂D₂ ⇒ 15 × 20 = M₂ × 12 ⇒ M₂ = 25 ⇒ 10 extra.
Answer: 10 men
Ex 4.5 A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish in 12 days. In how many days will A alone finish?
NORMAL METHOD: Efficiency A : B = 2 : 1. Total work = (2 + 1) × 12 = 36 units. A alone = 36/2 = 18 days.
SHORTCUT METHOD: Same as normal — efficiency ratio method is already the shortcut.
Answer: 18 days

Chapter 5: Time, Speed, Distance & Trainsसमय, चाल, दूरी एवं रेलगाड़ी  |  Useful for: NTPC • Group D • Technician • CGL • CHSL

VERY HIGH PRIORITY

1. Concept / अवधारणा

Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Train questions are special because the length of the train must be added to the distance covered.

चाल = दूरी ÷ समय। रेलगाड़ी के प्रश्नों में गाड़ी की लंबाई को भी दूरी में जोड़ना पड़ता है।

📐 FORMULA BOX / सूत्र
  • Speed = Distance / Time  |  Distance = Speed × Time  |  Time = Distance / Speed
  • km/h → m/s: multiply by 5/18  |  m/s → km/h: multiply by 18/5
  • Average speed (equal distances) = 2xy/(x + y)
  • Train crossing a pole / man = Length ÷ Speed
  • Train crossing a platform / bridge = (Train length + Platform length) ÷ Speed
  • Two trains in opposite directions: relative speed = S₁ + S₂
  • Two trains in same direction: relative speed = S₁ − S₂
  • Time to cross each other = (L₁ + L₂) / relative speed
⚡ 10-SECOND TRICKS / शॉर्टकट
  • 36 km/h = 10 m/s, 54 = 15, 72 = 20, 90 = 25, 108 = 30 (every +18 km/h adds 5 m/s). / हर 18 km/h पर 5 m/s बढ़ता है।
  • If time is fixed, Distance ∝ Speed. If distance is fixed, Speed ∝ 1/Time.
  • Late/early type: Distance = (S₁ × S₂)/(S₂ − S₁) × time difference (in hours).
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES / सामान्य गलतियाँ
  • Forgetting to add platform length to train length.
  • Mixing km/h and m/s in one equation.
  • Taking simple average of speeds for a round trip (use 2xy/(x+y)).
⚠️ EXAM TRAPS / परीक्षा जाल
  • 'Man running in same direction' ⇒ subtract speeds; 'opposite direction' ⇒ add speeds.
  • Crossing a pole or a standing man ⇒ platform length = 0.

2. Solved Examples — Normal vs Shortcut / हल उदाहरण

Ex 5.1 Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
NORMAL METHOD: 72 × 1000 / 3600 = 20 m/s.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
Answer: 20 m/s
Ex 5.2 A 150 m train crosses a pole in 10 s. Find its speed in km/h.
NORMAL METHOD: Speed = 150/10 = 15 m/s = 15 × 18/5 = 54 km/h.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 15 m/s = 54 km/h (table).
Answer: 54 km/h
Ex 5.3 A 200 m train at 54 km/h crosses a 250 m platform. Time taken?
NORMAL METHOD: Speed = 15 m/s. Distance = 200 + 250 = 450 m. Time = 450/15 = 30 s.
SHORTCUT METHOD: Same; speed conversion via 5/18 is the shortcut.
Answer: 30 s
Ex 5.4 A man goes at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find his average speed.
NORMAL METHOD: Let one-way distance = 120 km: time = 3 + 2 = 5 h; total 240 km ⇒ 48 km/h.
SHORTCUT METHOD: 2xy/(x+y) = 2×40×60/100 = 48 km/h.
Answer: 48 km/h
Ex 5.5 Two trains of 100 m and 150 m run in opposite directions at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. How long to cross each other?
NORMAL METHOD: Relative speed = 100 km/h = 250/9 m/s. Distance = 250 m ⇒ time = 250 ÷ (250/9) = 9 s.
SHORTCUT METHOD: Relative = 100 × 5/18 = 250/9 m/s ⇒ 250 ÷ 250/9 = 9 s.
Answer: 9 s

One-Page Revision Sheetएक पृष्ठ सूत्र-सार

1. Percentage
  • x% of y = (x × y)/100   |   x% of y = y% of x
  • Net change after +a% and +b% = a + b + (a×b)/100  (use minus sign for decrease)
  • Increase x%, then decrease to return to original = x/(100+x) × 100 %
  • Decrease x%, then increase to return to original = x/(100−x) × 100 %
2. Profit, Loss & Discount
  • Profit = SP − CP  |  Loss = CP − SP
  • Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100  |  Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
  • SP = CP × (100 + P%)/100  |  CP = SP × 100/(100 + P%)
  • For loss: SP = CP × (100 − L%)/100  |  CP = SP × 100/(100 − L%)
3. Simple & Compound Interest
  • SI = (P × R × T)/100  |  Amount A = P + SI
  • CI Amount = P (1 + R/100)n  |  CI = A − P
  • Half-yearly compounding: Rate = R/2, Time = 2n
  • Quarterly compounding: Rate = R/4, Time = 4n
4. Time & Work (with Pipes & Cisterns)
  • Work = Efficiency × Time
  • A and B together = (a × b)/(a + b) days
  • Efficiency ratio = inverse of time ratio
  • M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂ (men–days–hours chain)
5. Time, Speed, Distance & Trains
